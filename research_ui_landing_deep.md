## 2. Hero section — evidence struktura

### 2.1. Hero elementlari (151 SaaS hero tahlili — webanatomy)

151 ta hero section skoring natijasi (adoption % — qancha past bo'lsa, shuncha katta raqobat ustunligi):

| Element | Adoption | Izoh |
|---|---|---|
| Clear value proposition | 89% | "table stakes" — shartsiz |
| Visual hierarchy (H1→subhead→CTA) | 74% | tabiiy oqim |
| Product visual (real screenshot) | 68% | stock emas, real demo |
| Target audience clarity | 61% | "o'qituvchilar uchun" — 5 soniyada |
| Risk reducer near CTA | 42% | "no card", "bepul" — **katta imkoniyat** |
| Social proof above fold | 41% | logolar/stat — **imkoniyat** |
| Differentiation | 26% | "Unlike X" — **katta imkoniyat** |
| Outcome timeframe | 21% | "2 daqiqada natija" — **eng kam uchraydi** |

**Xulosa:** Edikit hero'sida "outcome timeframe" va "risk reducer" va "social proof" — raqobatchilardan ajralib turadigan joylar. Masalan: "3 daqiqada test yarating va sinf natijasini ko'ring" + "Bepul boshlash — karta talab qilinmaydi".

### 2.2. Hero 5 komponenti (omniconvert + alfdesigngroup + key-g)

```text
1. HEADLINE — bitta value proposition, outcome-based, <10 so'z
   (H1 = "Sinf nimani tushunganini shu zahoti ko'ring" — learning outcome)
2. SUBHEADLINE — 30 so'zdan kam, mexanizm/audience aniqligi
3. VISUAL — mahsulotni ko'rsatadi (real demo), WebP <200KB yoki video <2MB autoplay-muted
4. PRIMARY CTA — action verb + benefit ("Bepul boshlash"), bitta
5. SOCIAL PROOF — logo strip/stat/testimonial — 3 soniyada hazm bo'ladigan
```

Muhim qoidalar (key-g, alfdesigngroup):
- H1 6-9 so'z, birinchi qatorda eng kuchli value
- Hero balandligi 60-65vh desktop; mobile'da visual **matn ostida** (matn first)
- H1 mobile'da 2 qatordan oshmasin (3 qator CTA'ni pastga itaradi)
- Motion: subtle — parallax/fade; "motion supports message, not overpower" 
- Yagona CTA, secondary CTA aniq subordinate

### 2.3. CTA copy — evidence

- "Submit"/"Click here" — yo'qotilgan imkoniyat (alfdesigngroup)
- First-person CTA ("Start My Trial") second-person'dan yaxshiroq (involve, ivyforms: 10-90%)
- Specificity: "Start your free 14-day trial" — nima olasan, qancha, nima qilasan (alfdesigngroup)
- Context-specific: "Continue to Review" > "Continue" (medium microcopy)
- Color: CTA sahifadagi eng vibrant rang; red CTA generic'dan 200% ko'p click (saashero — lekin eslatma: rang psixologiyasi kontekstga bog'liq, Edikit'da bu Signal Cyan/Insight Amber bo'ladi, "red" emas)
- Trust microcopy ostida: "No credit card required" (memorable.design)

### 2.4. CTA placement (5 optimal pozitsiya — saashero + hoopstudio)

1. **Above-fold hero** — "Get Demo" 5 soniyalik clarity
2. **After social proof** — logo/testimonial'dan keyin (risk kamayadi)
3. **Sticky (long-scroll)** — mobile'da thumb zone
4. **Mid-page** — benefit section'dan keyin
5. **Footer anchor** — oxirigacha o'qiganlar uchun

### 2.5. Directional cues

- F/Z pattern — CTA ko'z tabiiy tushadigan joyda (invespcro)
- Headline → subheadline → CTA tabiiy oqim; keraksiz chalg'itish yo'q

### Nav qoidalari (brilliantdirectories, logoswebdesigns, ohio.edu, iowa.gov)

- **5-7 item max** (NN/g — short-term memory; 7 dan oshsa "hunting" boshlanadi)
- Label: qisqa, aniq, 1-2 so'z, ≤15 belgi (iowa.gov davlat standarti)
- Sticky header: conversion lift 3-7%, e-commerce'da 25% (cart bilan)
- Mobile: hybrid — 3-5 kritik item ko'rinadi + hamburger secondary (NN/g/CXL)
- Landing'da nav minimal bo'ladi (unbounce: "remove navigation and other distractions")
- Breadcrumbs: auth/panel'da foydali (SearchPilot +5% organik), landing'da shart emas

## 4. Education landing'ga xos (lapa.ninja + instapage + CXL)

Education landing samarali elementlari (lapa.ninja):
1. Clear value proposition — ta'lim benefit'i darhol
2. Visual showcase — real demo (not abstract)
3. Pain-point messaging — o'qituvchi muammosi
4. Social proof — institut/testimonial (ruxsat bilan)
5. Trust signals — credential, security, "data UZ'da"
6. Mobile optimization — 70%+ mobile conversions
7. Clear navigation, single goal

Ta'lim framing (instapage/CXL):
- **Learning-goal headline** vs platform-framing ("tushunishni ko'rish" > "test platformasi")
- **Low-risk CTA**: "Bepul boshlash" / "Demo" — skill-building framing
- **Curriculum transparency** = trust (CXL) — "qanday ishlaydi" section aniq
- **Career/outcome-driven** — "natija" birinchi

Konversion benchmark (education): organic 8-15%, paid 3-10%, free trial 10-25% (lapa.ninja)

## 5. Motion spec (landing uchun)

### 5.1. Hozirgi xatolar (index.ejs) → tuzatish

| Hozirgi | Muammo | Yechim |
|---|---|---|
| `orbitL/orbitS` (aylanuvchi) | Doimiy harakat — chalg'itadi, gaming/detskiy signal | O'chirish; hero'da faqat product frame |
| `drift` (suzuvchi blobs) | style.md 41: "no blurred blobs" | O'chirish |
| `pulseAura` (glow pulse) | Diqqatni mahsulotdan oladi | O'chirish; kontrastli CTA kifoya |
| `shimmerBtn` | Marketing texnikasi; professional emas | Oddiy hover (100-160ms) |
| `countUp` | Statistikaga sun'iy urg'u; fake sonlar trustni buzadi | Tekshiriladigan stat bo'lsa — sokin animation; yo'q bo'lsa — yo'q |
| `revealCard` | Scroll reveal — jiddiy section'lar uchun cheklangan | Faqat product proof'da bir marta, 240-320ms |
| `float3d` | 3D suzish — detskiy | O'chirish |

### 5.2. Landing motion — yangi qoidalar (style.md 6 bilan mos)

- **Hero**: motion deyarli yo'q; product frame static; CTA hover 100ms; scroll cue subtle (opacity 120ms)
- **Scroll reveal**: faqat 2-3 jiddiy section (brand story, product proof) — 240-320ms, `prefers-reduced-motion`'da yo'q
- **View Transitions**: landing→login/register o'tishida crossfade 240-320ms (progressiv kuchaytirish)
- **Numbers**: tekshiriladigan stat bo'lsa, count-up 500ms bir marta
- **Hamma motion** `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` ichida
